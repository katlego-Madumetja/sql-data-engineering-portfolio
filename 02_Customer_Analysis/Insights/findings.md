 ## How many customers are inactive and what regions are they in ?

All 15 registered customers have made at least one purchase there are currently no inactive customers in the database. 
This is a healthy sign for the business, though monitoring for inactivity should be built into future reporting cycles

---
## Which Segment - Retail or Corporate - Drives more revenue ?

Corporate customers generate significantly more total revenue  (R156,473) compared to Retail (R120,007), despite Retail having 
more transactions (18 vs 12). Corporate customers spend more per transaction on average (R13,039 vs R6,667) — suggesting the 
business should prioritise corporate account retention as a revenue protection strategy.

## Which region has the highest average customer spend? 

Western Cape customers have the highest average spend per transaction at R12,823 — above KZN (R11,134) and Gauteng
(R9,860). This suggests Western Cape customers are either purchasing higher-value products or represent a more premium
customer base worth targeting with exclusive offerings.
