## Project 1 : Sales Analysis - RetailEdge SA completed

Business Scenario

You have been hired as a Junior Data Engineer at RetailEdge SA — a South African retail company with stores across three regions.

The sales manager has come to you with this brief:

"We have 6 months of transaction data sitting in a raw table. We need to understand which products are selling, which regions are performing, how revenue trends month by month, and who our top customers are. Can you clean the data, build the analysis, and give us something we can act on?"
